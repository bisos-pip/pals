Last updated: 2026-10-19 (the .here names and the PALS-BXO/CBM model added; nothing committed).

* Current State
  - bisos.pals now has the current PALS model alongside its earlier PALS-BPO code (kept, under review):
    =.here= names for PALS sites, the generic files a PALS site's =cbm/= links to, and =palsOfSys.cs=
    for a platform's PALS-BXOs, their CBMs and the platform's CBM leaves. Built 2026-10-18/19 while
    making =libre-halaal.org.here= the first PALS site; that work was planned in its own WorkPlan:
    =bro_rawBisos/bro_rawBisosPals/bySmb/libreHalaalFoundation/web/astro/AI-WorkPlan.org= (2.1, 2.2, 2.5,
    2.6), whose Results are the detailed record.
  - README.org rewritten (2.6): the model, its figure, Installation, Usage, Key Files.
  - Installed editable in bisos3 (was a PyPI copy, 0.16). =palsHereInfo.cs= and =palsOfSys.cs= are
    devbin symlinks made by hand: setup.py (Blee-generated) lists none of the new files.

* Architecture
  - =py3/bisos/pals/palsHere.py= --- the =.here= list =/bisos/var/palsHere/uri.list= (127.0.55.x,
    assigned once, stored, never reused), =palsHereIpOf()=, =palsDirOfPlant()= / =uriHereOf()= (a
    plant's PALS: the nearest directory up with =uri/here/value=, path never resolved), =plantOf()=,
    =dnsCapSetup()=. =palsHere_csu.py= / =bin/palsHereInfo.cs=: palsForEtcHosts, palsHereList,
    palsHereIpOf, palsHereAdd.
  - =py3/bisos/pals/palsOfSys.py= --- =/bisos/platform/sys/pals/<palsBxo>/{path,origin}/value= (host),
    =<palsBxo>/sys/cbm/<cbm>/{capabilityType,cbsSpec}/value= (repo; cbsSpec relative), and
    =leavesPlant()= into =/bisos/platform/sys/cbm/pals/<palsBxo>/<cbm>/= plus the realm's
    =ftoBranchProc.spcs=. =palsOfSys_csu.py= / =bin/palsOfSys.cs=: palsList, palsAdd, palsCbmsList,
    palsCbmAdd, palsLeavesPlant.
  - =py3/bin/= generic files, symlinked into a PALS site's =cbm/=: =pals-cbs.spcs= (CBS: no sbom, no
    units), =pals-assemble.cs= (siteBuild via astroProc.spcs, hereDns, webVirtDom),
    =pals-here-dns.spcs= (on bisos.dnsCap's seed), =pals-wvd.spcs= (on bisos.webCap's capWebVd seed).
  - =py3/images/palsModel-graphviz.pcs= --- the model's figure (one named graph), rendered beside it.
  - Uses, imported lazily: bisos.dnsCap, bisos.csSeed (palsHere.dnsCapSetup); bisos.capability,
    bisos.webCap (the generic files).

* What Works
  Verified on one Debian 13 host, 2026-10-18/19, with =libre-halaal.org.here=:
  - 16 checks of palsHere.py and 21 of palsOfSys.py on scratch trees (scripts were in the session's
    scratchpad, not kept: worth turning into =py3/tests/=).
  - The name resolves (127.0.55.1) through the palsHere block of /etc/hosts; the site is served.
  - =palsOfSys.cs= on the real platform: legacy entry migrated, CBM declared, leaf planted (gate
    available); the walker finds it; with the gate enabled for a test, leaf -> pals-cbs.spcs ->
    pals-assemble.cs ran end to end.

* Key Design Decisions
  - Recorded in the libreHalaalFoundation/web/astro AI-WorkPlan.org (2.1 .here names, 2.2 generic .spcs
    files, 2.5 the PALS-BXO/CBM model, user's leaf layout =pals/<palsBxo>/<cbm>/=) and summarized in
    README.org. Not repeated here.
  - A PALS-BXO declares; the platform holds state (list, gates, status logs).
  - Generic files are =.spcs=: they take their parameters from where they are planted, through symlinks;
    no path is resolved anywhere on that route (see the bisos.capability fix below).
  - =palsForEtcHosts= sets no opResults: the framework would print them into the /etc/hosts dblock.
  - Depends on fixes made in other repos at the same time (uncommitted): bisos.dnsCap
    (=hostsDblockCommands=, =preUpdateFuncs=) and bisos.capability (=resolveCbsSpec= no longer follows
    symlinks; fullyMaterialize walks =sys/cbm/pals=). Commit those first.
  - Out of chain: AI-Outputs.org (blee-binders/evolution) entries on the dnsCap block, the CBS-symlink
    fix and sysCbmManage's bare ftoBranchProc.spcs.
